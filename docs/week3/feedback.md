## Self-review of Personas and Wireframes

Screen 1 | Frames: join-phone, join-laptop

**Works well:** The PWD / Priority button is the biggest thing on both frames and takes one tap, so Pet can start in 5 seconds.

**Problem:** Anyone can tap PWD / Priority, and there’s no way to undo it. A wrong tap prints a priority ticket in the wrong queue (error prevention).

**Change (kept):** Instead of just doing one tap in the category button, i will put another button to proceed in the next part so they can change if they mistouch the priority button. Also, I will put the priority button below the regular button to lessen the human error. Even though the cashier can check the category.

Screen 2 | Frames: my-place-phone, my-place-laptop

**Works well:** The ticket number is the biggest thing on the phone and position is right under it, so Pet sees what she needs first. The overhead display puts NOW SERVING in huge type, which matches my noisy, crowded pharmacy rule.

**Problem:** The phone says “Live” even if Pet’s signal drops, so she could be looking at old info without knowing (visibility of status). The wording also jumps between “number” and “ticket”.

**Change (kept):** None. The ticket number would be still there even there is no internet connection.

Screen 3 | dashboard-phone, dashboard-laptop

**Works well:** all Next Priority is the biggest button on both frames. It’s top-left on the laptop and in the bottom thumb zone on the phone, so DocG can hit it one-handed.

**Problem:** “Call next regular” sits right under it on the phone. A mis-tap while she’s handling medicine calls the wrong customer (error prevention).

**Change (kept):** None. The size difference keeps the two buttons apart, and their would be a colored button design that would differentiate the two.
